### **PROJECT BRIEF \- FASHION BRAND**

### **User**

Young adults, students, and working professionals who want to look stylish, confident, and well-dressed without spending too much money.

### **Problem**

Many customers struggle to find clothing that is **fashionable, affordable, good quality, and suitable for their personal style**. They may also find it difficult to identify a clothing brand they can trust for consistent quality and a convenient shopping experience.

### **Main Journey**

The customer should be able to **discover the clothing brand, browse available collections, choose styles and sizes, view product details, place an order, make payment, and receive their clothes conveniently**. The goal is to give customers a simple and enjoyable shopping experience while helping them find clothing that matches their style and budget.
